Garbage disposal repair in Rosemead, CA

Garbage disposal repair in Rosemead covers jams, humming, leaks and units that will not turn on. Many problems are fixable, and a few are a sign that it is time for a new disposal.

We test the motor, clear the jam, check the seals and drain connections and tell you whether repair or replacement makes sense.A hum without spinning usually means a jam, and a hex key on the base can free it with the power off.

Code and standards

The California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), covers traps, drain connections and waste fittings under a kitchen sink. We check each connection when we repair or replace a disposal.

Common disposal problems

Humming but not spinning

Usually a jam. The motor is trying and failing to turn.

Nothing happens

A tripped reset button or a dead motor.

Leaks at the bottom or sides

Seals or housing have failed.

Bad smells

Food residue and grease coat the chamber and drain.

What to try before you call

Turn off the power at the switch and the breaker. Look under the unit for a hex socket in the bottom center and turn it with a hex key to free a jam, then press the red reset button on the base.

If it still hums or trips, stop there. Repeated resetting can burn out the motor, and a leak means the housing may have failed.

Choosing a replacement unit

A half horsepower unit handles light cooking for one or two people. Three quarter and one horsepower units grind tougher scraps and run quieter, which suits busy family kitchens.

Check the mounting style so the new unit fits your sink flange, and think about whether you want a continuous feed or batch feed model. We bring common sizes and can match the existing connections.

We also check the dishwasher drain connection and the trap, because a poor connection there is a common source of leaks that get blamed on the disposal.

How long a disposal should last

Most disposals last about eight to twelve years when used correctly. A unit that has been replaced several times in a decade usually points to misuse or a drain problem downstream, not bad luck.

If yours is nearing that age and starts leaking or tripping, replacement is often wiser than paying for several repairs.

Keeping a disposal healthy

Run cold water while the disposal works, and avoid grease, coffee grounds in large amounts, fibrous peels and bones. Citrus peels and ice cubes can freshen the chamber in moderation.

If water backs up from the disposal into the second sink, the line beyond the trap may be partly blocked.
