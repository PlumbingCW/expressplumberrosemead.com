Sewer camera inspection in Rosemead, CA

A sewer camera inspection in Rosemead lets us see inside your line instead of guessing. A small waterproof camera travels through the pipe and shows cracks, roots, sags, offsets and buildup on a live screen.

It is useful before buying a house, after repeat backups or before an expensive repair. If the camera finds a blockage we can clear with sewer line cleaning.

Code and standards

Many agencies describe sewer pipe defects using the NASSCO PACP coding system, an industry standard. It is reasonable to ask any contractor how they describe and record what the camera finds.

What the camera can show you

Roots

Fine roots at joints or thick masses that block flow.

Cracks and breaks

Fractures, missing sections and collapsed pipe.

Sags or bellies

Low spots where water and waste collect.

Buildup

Grease, scale and debris narrowing the pipe.

Reading the findings

We explain each finding in plain language and tell you which items need action and which can wait. A hairline crack with no roots is very different from a collapsed section, and the plan changes accordingly.

You decide what to do next. We give you the footage distance and depth for every problem so any contractor can find it, including ones you ask for a second opinion.

Inspections for buyers and sellers

A home inspector usually cannot see inside the sewer line. If you are buying a house built decades ago, a camera look can reveal roots, cracks and sags before you own the problem.

Sellers sometimes order one too, so they can fix an issue or disclose it up front. Either way, the footage gives both sides a factual basis.

Ask for a written summary of the findings if you plan to negotiate a purchase price or get repair quotes from other companies.

What you get afterward

After the inspection we explain what we saw, mark where problems are and give a clear price for any repair. If the line is healthy, you leave with that knowledge and a baseline to compare against later.

A clean report is as valuable as a bad one. It tells you that a line is not the cause of your problems and lets you look elsewhere.
