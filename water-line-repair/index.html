Water line repair in Rosemead, CA

Water line repair in Rosemead covers the pipe that runs from the meter at the street to your home. A leak here can waste water, reduce pressure and soak your yard, and you may not see it for weeks.

We find the leak, check the pipe condition and offer repair or replacement. For leaks inside the house, see leak detection.

Code and standards

The California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), requires a pressure regulator when static water pressure is above 80 psi. We check pressure at the house while we are repairing the line.

Signs of a failing water line

Low pressure

A drop in pressure through the whole house can mean a leak in the supply line.

Wet spots in the yard

Soggy or sunken ground with no sprinkler on.

Dirty or discolored water

Corroded pipe can release rust and sediment.

High water bill

Unexplained usage often signals a leak.

Who owns which part of the line

Rosemead is served by several water providers, including Golden State Water, San Gabriel Valley Water and California American Water, as listed by the City. The utility generally owns the line up to the meter, and the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the house.

Check your bill to see which provider serves you. If the leak is before the meter, call the provider. If it is after, we can repair it.

What happens during a repair

We locate the line, mark the dig area and call for utility markings where needed. Then we expose the line, make the repair or run the replacement and test under pressure before backfilling.

Landscaping, concrete and pavers over the line can be restored by us or a contractor of your choice, and we tell you in advance what is practical.

Low pressure and the water line

Not every pressure drop means a leak. A partly closed valve, a failing pressure regulator or a clogged filter can all reduce flow, so we test pressure at the hose bib before we look at the line.

If pressure is too high, it can stress pipes and fittings. A regulator keeps it in a safe range, and we can check yours during the visit.
