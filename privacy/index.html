Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-16.

What we collect

We collect the information you give us when you call or email, such as your name, phone number, address and a description of the plumbing problem. We use it to schedule and perform service and to answer your questions.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Website visits

This website does not have forms. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages visited, for security and performance. We do not sell personal information.

Third party content on our pages, such as embedded maps and fonts, may set their own cookies or collect usage data under their own policies.

Sharing and retention

We share information only with people who help us serve you, such as the technician assigned to your visit, or when the law requires it. We keep records as long as needed for service, warranty and legal reasons.
