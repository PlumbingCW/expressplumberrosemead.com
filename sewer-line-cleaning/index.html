Sewer line cleaning in Rosemead, CA

Sewer line cleaning in Rosemead removes the blockage in the pipe between your house and the public sewer. We use a cable machine or hydro jetting depending on the clog, then run water to confirm the line flows freely.

A main line problem shows up in several fixtures at once, which separates it from a single sink clog. For single fixtures, see drain cleaning.

Code and standards

The California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), calls for cleanouts in a building sewer, and that access point is what lets us clear the line without opening the ground.

Main line backed up? What to do right now

Waste rising in a tub, shower or floor drain is an emergency. Call us any hour and follow these steps.

Stop water use

Pause laundry, dishes, showers and flushing.

Locate your cleanout

A capped pipe outside near the house or property line is usually the access point.

Do not pour chemicals

They rarely reach the clog and can harm the work crew.

Check the street

If neighbors also have backups, the public sewer may be blocked. Call 800-675-4357.

Why main lines block

Tree roots

Roots enter at joints and grow into dense masses.

Grease and wipes

Grease coats pipe walls and wipes do not break down.

Pipe sags

Low spots hold waste and collect debris.

Scale and age

Older cast iron roughens and narrows over time.

Roots and your sewer line

Roots go where water and nutrients are. Tiny cracks and open joints in an older clay or cast iron line let moisture out, and fine roots follow it in and grow into a mass.

Cutting roots clears the line, but they grow back unless the joint is repaired or lined. If your line clogs every year or two, ask us about a camera look and a lasting fix.

What the visit looks like

A technician arrives, finds the cleanout and tests drainage. We then run the cable or jetter, watch what comes out and flush the line with water to confirm the flow.

We clean up the work area, show you what we found and give you a clear price for any follow up. If we think a camera check is wise, we will explain why.

After the line is clear

We run water through the line and check that every fixture drains. If the blockage came from roots or grease, we tell you what we saw and whether we suggest a camera check or a follow up cleaning.

Keep wipes, paper towels and grease out of the drain, and avoid planting thirsty trees near the line. Those two habits prevent most repeat main line clogs.
