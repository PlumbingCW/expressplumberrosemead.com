Water heater replacement in Rosemead, CA

If your tank is leaking at the base, rusting through or producing rusty hot water, water heater replacement in Rosemead is usually the safe choice. A tank that has failed inside cannot be patched, and waiting can turn a small drip into a flooded garage or utility closet.

We remove the old heater, set the new one to current code and test the gas or electric connections before we leave. You get a clear price before work starts, so the decision is yours.

Code and standards

The California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), sets the rules for relief valves, discharge piping and venting on a new heater. California Health and Safety Code section 19211 separately covers earthquake bracing.

Tank leaking or no hot water? Act now

Water pooling around the tank is the urgent case. Gas smell is more urgent still, so leave the house and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 or 911 before you call us.

Shut off the cold supply

Turn the cold water valve on top of the heater clockwise until it stops. This slows the leak.

Cut power or gas

Switch an electric unit off at the breaker. For gas, turn the control dial to off or pilot.

Do not drain it yet

Leave the water in the tank until we arrive. Hot water can scald and a drain hose can leak.

Protect the floor

Move stored boxes and use towels or a wet vac if water is spreading.

How to tell repair from replacement

Age and condition decide it. A heater that is past its expected life, leaking from the tank itself or needing repeated parts is a replacement job. A newer unit with a single failed part is usually worth repairing, which our water heater repair page covers.

California rules that shape the install

California law requires water heaters to be braced or anchored against earthquakes, under Health and Safety Code section 19211. We strap every tank we install and can check the strapping on your current one. Replacement also generally needs a permit, so ask the City of Rosemead building division about its current process.

Gas units need correct venting and combustion air, and electric units need a properly sized circuit. If you are weighing a different type, see tankless water heaters or the fuller install steps on water heater installation.

Hard water and older tanks

Rosemead is served by several water providers, so your water quality depends on your address. Check your provider's annual water quality report for hardness, because hard water leaves scale in a tank and shortens its life. A water softener is worth discussing if your report shows high hardness.
