Hills, slopes and plumbing

Monterey Park has hilly areas, including the Garvey and Monterey Hills. Homes on slopes can have steeper drain runs and longer water lines, and water pressure can vary with elevation.

If your pressure is high at the bottom of a hill, a pressure regulator protects fixtures and fittings. If it is low, it is worth a check for a leak or a failing valve, see water line repair.
