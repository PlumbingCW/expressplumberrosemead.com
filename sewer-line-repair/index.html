Sewer line repair in Rosemead, CA

Sewer line repair in Rosemead is for the pipe that carries waste from your house to the public sewer. When that line cracks, collapses or fills with roots, a cable can only buy you a few weeks of relief.

We confirm the damage with a camera, locate the problem depth and explain the repair options so you can compare them. A line that only needs cleaning is better served by sewer line cleaning.

Code and standards

The California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), sets material and cleanout requirements for sewer lines, and permits for work near the street are issued locally. We explain which apply to your address.

Sewage backing up? What to do right now

Sewage coming up through floor drains or tubs is an emergency and a health risk. Call us any hour and use these steps.

Stop all water use

No toilets, showers or laundry until the line is open.

Keep everyone away

Children and pets should stay out of affected rooms.

Report public sewer problems

If the main in the street looks blocked, Los Angeles County Public Works takes urgent reports at 800-675-4357.

Open a window

Ventilation helps with odor while you wait.

Signs of a failing sewer line

Repeat backups

Clogs that return after cleaning point to a structural problem.

Sewage smell in the yard

Damp patches and odor near the line suggest a leak.

Sinkholes or soft spots

Soil washing into a broken pipe can leave dips in the lawn.

Slow drains throughout

Several fixtures draining poorly together suggests the main line is restricted.

What we find in older Rosemead lines

About 51.6 percent of Rosemead homes were built before 1960, based on the City of Rosemead Housing Element. Lines from that era are often clay, cast iron or fiber pipe, and each can crack, offset at joints or let roots in.

Ownership matters. Los Angeles County Public Works maintains Rosemead's public sewers, while the pipe from your house to the public main is generally the owner's. A camera look shows which side the problem sits on.

Public sewer or septic tank

Most Rosemead homes connect to the public sewer. The county maintains the main under the street, and the owner is responsible for the lateral that runs from the house to that main.

If you own a property with an older septic tank or a cesspool, tell the dispatcher on the first call. Pumping, a failing leach field and a sewer lateral problem need different work, and we want to send the right equipment.

Questions to ask any sewer contractor

Ask to see the camera footage that shows the problem, and ask where on the line the damage is. A trustworthy contractor will show you the screen and give you the footage distance.

Ask which options fit your pipe and what each one involves for your yard or driveway. Then compare written scopes, because a vague price makes comparison impossible.
