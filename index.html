What to do before the plumber arrives

A few quick actions limit damage in any emergency. Know where your main shutoff valve is, because closing it stops most active leaks. If you cannot find it, tell the dispatcher and we will talk you through it.

For a backup, stop running water and keep people and pets away from the affected area. For a leaking water heater, switch off the power or gas and close the cold supply valve on top of the tank.

Take photos of any damage before you clean up. They help if you decide to contact your insurer. Our water heater replacement and sewer line repair pages explain what each emergency involves.
