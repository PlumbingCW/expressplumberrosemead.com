How we confirm service for your address

The dispatcher asks for your street address, your ZIP code and the nearest cross street. We check that against where our crews are and tell you what we can offer. If the address is in one of the towns above, we normally can help.

Service availability is never a guess. If we cannot reach you in a reasonable time, we say so, and we do not make a promise we cannot keep.

You can also ask the dispatcher about planned work such as a water heater swap or a camera inspection. Those visits can be scheduled around your day and priced before they start.

Planning ahead for an emergency

Write down your address, your main shutoff location and our number where everyone in the house can find it. If you rent, add your landlord contact. In an emergency, the faster you can describe the problem, the faster we can help.

Keep a flashlight, a bucket and old towels near the water heater and under the kitchen sink. Those basic items limit damage while a plumber is on the way.
