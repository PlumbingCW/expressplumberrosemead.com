Faucet repair in Rosemead, CA

Faucet repair in Rosemead usually means replacing a worn cartridge, washer, seal or aerator. A drip may seem harmless, but it wastes water and can stain a sink and rot the cabinet below.

We repair kitchen, bathroom, tub and utility faucets, and we tell you when a faucet is too worn to be worth fixing.Cost depends on the faucet type, the part and the access, as the price section below explains.

Code and standards

According to the EPA WaterSense program, labeled bathroom faucets flow at no more than 1.5 gallons per minute. A new aerator or faucet can cut water use if yours runs much higher.

Valves that will not shut off

A supply valve under a sink or behind a toilet that will not turn, or that keeps dripping after it closes, is a common reason people call. Old stop valves seize with mineral buildup, and forcing one can snap the stem and start a leak.

If water is already flowing, close the main shutoff for the house and call. Replacing a worn stop valve is usually a short visit, and it gives you a working shutoff for the next time you need one.

Shower and tub faucets

A dripping tub spout or a shower that will not stop running usually points to a worn cartridge or diverter. Water that keeps running wastes money and can stain the tub.

A shower valve that leaks inside the wall shows up as a stain on the next room or a soft spot in the drywall. Tell us where you see the damage, because it changes how we open the wall.

A quick checklist before you call

Check whether the leak is at the spout, the handle or underneath the sink. Each points to a different part, so telling us where the water shows up helps us bring the right cartridge or seal.

Find the brand name on the faucet body or handle if you can. Many manufacturers use proprietary cartridges, and knowing the brand speeds up the repair.

Choosing a replacement faucet

If we recommend replacement, look for solid brass bodies and ceramic disc cartridges, which tend to outlast plastic parts. Check the finish too, since cheap finishes pit and peel in hard water areas.

Match the number of mounting holes in your sink and the reach and height you need for the basin. Bring those details when you shop and we can confirm fit.

We install the new faucet and test the shutoff valves while we are under the sink, replacing worn supply lines at the same time so you do not have a second leak a year from now.

Where water goes when a faucet leaks

A slow drip at a kitchen faucet can run down the supply lines into the cabinet and soak the base for weeks without anyone noticing. Particleboard cabinet floors swell and crumble when wet, and the damp space is a perfect spot for mold.

Open the cabinet every few months and feel the supply lines and shutoff valves with a dry paper towel. Moisture there is a warning that a repair is overdue, even if the faucet itself looks fine.

Hard water and faucets

Water hardness varies by provider in Rosemead, so check your annual water quality report. Hard water leaves scale on aerators and cartridges, which causes low flow and drips. Soaking an aerator in vinegar can help, and a water softener reduces the problem for the whole house.
