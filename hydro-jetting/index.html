Hydro jetting in Rosemead, CA

Hydro jetting in Rosemead uses a hose and a nozzle that sprays water at high pressure inside the pipe. The water cuts through grease, soap scale and light roots and flushes the debris out, leaving the pipe wall cleaner than a cable can.

It is a good fit for repeat clogs, restaurant style grease problems and older lines with heavy buildup. A cable is quicker for a simple blockage, and our drain cleaning team will tell you which makes sense.

Code and standards

The California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), calls for cleanouts at set points in a drain system, which is what gives a jetting hose a place to enter the line.

Why jetting suits older homes

About 51.6 percent of Rosemead homes were built before 1960 per the City of Rosemead Housing Element. Original drain lines in homes that old are often cast iron or clay, and their rough interiors hold scale and grease.

Jetting washes that buildup away. Before we start, a sewer camera check confirms the pipe is sound enough, because jetting is not the right tool for a collapsed or badly cracked line.

How a jetting visit goes

We start with a camera look when the history is unclear, then feed the jetting hose through a cleanout. The nozzle works back and forth through the line while the water carries debris to a point we can recover it.

We finish with a flow test and a recommendation for how often the line should be cleaned, if at all. Our sewer line cleaning page covers the main line in more detail.

Preventing the next backup

Most grease problems start in the kitchen. Wipe pans before washing, keep cooking oil out of the sink and use a strainer. In older lines, a camera look every few years shows whether roots or scale are building before they cause a backup.

If you manage a small business with a kitchen, regular jetting on a schedule is often cheaper than repeated emergency visits. See commercial plumbing for what we offer.
