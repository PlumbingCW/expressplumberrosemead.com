Before We Arrive

A few steps protect your home while a technician is on the way. Water or sewage damage grows by the minute, so act first and call right after.

Find the main shutoff

Turn it clockwise to stop a burst or leaking pipe. If you cannot find it, tell the dispatcher.

Stop using drains

If waste is backing up, stop flushing and running water until we clear the line.

Switch off the water heater

For a leak near the tank, cut power or gas and close the cold supply.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and valuables off wet floors and give us room to work.

Older homes and El Monte's history

El Monte calls itself the End of the Santa Fe Trail and was incorporated in 1912. In the 1930s a New Deal homestead project helped build single-family ranch houses here, so parts of the city have housing that is close to ninety years old.

Houses of that age often carry original drain lines and older supply pipe. If you live in one and have repeat clogs or low pressure, a camera inspection or a conversation about repiping can save repeat visits.

Hard water, appliances and your bills

Water quality varies across the area, so check your provider report for hardness. Scale shortens the life of water heaters, faucets and dishwashers, and a softener can pay back over time if the report shows hard water.

If you notice white crust on fixtures, spotty dishes or a heater that rumbles, mention it when you call. It helps us choose the right fix, whether that is a flush, a part or a softener.

Water and sewer questions in El Monte

Water suppliers and sewer maintenance differ across the area, so check your bill or your city public works office to see who serves your address. Their urgent line handles problems in the street, and we handle the plumbing on your property.

If you are not sure which side the problem is on, call us. A camera look can show whether a blockage sits on your private line or beyond it.
