What a call with us looks like

A person answers, day or night. We ask for your address, the nearest cross street and what the problem is doing, because the answers tell us how urgent it is and what to bring.

We then tell you what we can offer, usually the earliest same-day appointment when available, and how the price will work. We do not promise an arrival time we cannot control, and we would rather tell you the honest window.

When the technician arrives, they look at the problem, explain the cause and give you a clear price before work starts. You say yes or no, and the work begins only after you agree.

How we keep our website honest

We do not publish made up reviews, awards or numbers. We do not name people on this site, and we do not claim an arrival time. What we do claim is that the phone line is open 24/7, that we are licensed and insured and that you get a clear price before work starts.

You can verify any California contractor license on the Contractors State License Board website. We encourage you to do that for any contractor you hire, including us.

Plumbing safety we repeat on every page

A few safety points are worth repeating. If you smell gas, leave the house and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 or 911 before you do anything else. If water is near electrical outlets or the panel, stay away and call an electrician as well.

For a leak, close the main shutoff valve. For a backup, stop using water. Those simple steps limit damage, and they give the plumber a better starting point when we arrive.

We would rather you call early about a small problem than late about a large one. A short call costs nothing, and the dispatcher can tell you whether the problem can wait for a daytime visit.
