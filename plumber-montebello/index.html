From oil fields to housing

Montebello was once oil country. Standard Oil found oil in the Montebello hills in 1917, and wells later covered the hills for decades. Much of the housing and the pipe in the ground predates modern materials.

Older pipe and a history of heavy ground use are good reasons to check old lines before they fail. A camera inspection is a fast way to see what is under your property.

Rental properties and shared lines

Montebello has many renters and many small multi-unit properties. Shared sewer lines can back up into the lowest unit, and tenants may not control the repair.

We work with landlords and tenants and give a written scope so the decision can be made quickly. For larger properties, see commercial plumbing.

What older Montebello properties tend to need

Properties near older commercial streets such as Whittier Boulevard often have mixed plumbing from several remodels. Galvanized supply stubs may be joined to copper, and drain lines may change material under the floor.

Joints between different materials are where leaks start. If you see green or white crust at a pipe joint, call us before it fails.
